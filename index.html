How it works

Features
Each image is reduced to 22 numbers: hue histogram, saturation and brightness, gold-shine share, edge strength, direction of strokes and a Fourier measure of pattern repetition.
Model
A Random Forest (scikit-learn) classifies the features into six crafts. KMeans finds the dominant colour palette.
Training data
This demo is trained on generated pattern images, so it recognises the sample images perfectly but real photos are less certain. Add real photos to dataset/<craft>/ and rebuild to improve it.
UN goals
SDG 4 (education about heritage), SDG 8 (artisan livelihoods), SDG 11 (protecting cultural heritage), SDG 12 (responsible consumption of authentic crafts).
